## Journey model (all clinical roles)

Atlas moves enrollees through **Regulation → Readiness → Renewal**. Gates, regression, and the strip map are documented in [journey-phases.md](./journey-phases.md).

T2B, T3A, T4, and T4+ accounts that can open the warm line also get a Pray Phone icon in the top bar, left of Account Settings, on every menu. The green number is how many calls are waiting in the conferencer queue. Zero means that queue is empty. A press opens the agent console. A kiosk call turns the icon red and rings the browser. The icon is shown when the account passes warm-line access. The conferencer address is built in. Partners do not get the icon or the menu. Day-to-day steps are in [navigator-guide.md](./navigator-guide.md). Training steps are in [../prayphone/interaction-procedures.md](../prayphone/interaction-procedures.md). The IT runbook is [../it/prayphone.md](../it/prayphone.md).

## Important access rule

Signing in determines what data you can see. The on-screen **role switcher** only changes menus and layout. It does **not** expand your data access. Information Technology (IT) enforces access with Row-Level Security (RLS) on your signed-in identity.
